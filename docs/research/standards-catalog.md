# Patent Standards Catalog — China (CN) and United States (US)

Catalog of the authoritative texts (专利标准 / patent standards) that govern patent
drafting and examination in China and the United States: what exists, which edition is
current, who issues it, where the official text lives, and how to cite it.

- Scope: official names (Chinese and English), current edition/revision, governing
  institution, canonical official location (URL), what it governs, and stable citation
  anchors (part/chapter/section numbering usable in output citations).
- Method: **primary sources only** — official government websites (flk.npc.gov.cn,
  npc.gov.cn, gov.cn, cnipa.gov.cn, uscode.house.gov, govinfo.gov, ecfr.gov, uspto.gov).
  No secondary write-ups. Every factual claim below carries its source URL.
- Verified on 2026-08-10 (all URLs re-checked at retrieval time).

**Cross-system note for citation practice:** CN patent writing citations point to
article numbers of 专利法 and 实施细则 (e.g., 专利法第26条第3款 — 权利要求书; 实施细则第20条 — 说明书)
and 指南 part/chapter/section (e.g., 指南 第二部分第二章 — 说明书和权利要求书). US citations point to
statute section (35 U.S.C. §112), rule (37 CFR §1.71), and MPEP section (MPEP §2106,
§2164, §2171-2176). The 2023 CN package (专利法 decision + 实施细则修订 + 审查指南2023 +
过渡办法) took effect as one body on 2024-01-20.
