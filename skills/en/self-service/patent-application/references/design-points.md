# Design Interview and View Checklist

Standards pointer: `../../patent-standards/references/cn-design.md`.

This branch is for appearance protection. It does not use the invention/utility-model four elements and does not generate method claims, a technical specification, or algorithm formulas.

## Interview

1. What is the product name and intended use?
2. Which visual features are protected: overall shape, partial shape, pattern, color, or a combination?
3. Which faces or areas carry the design points?
4. Are there similar designs to include in the same application? Which one is the basic design?
5. Is color protection requested? Are the supplied images or photographs consistent with that choice?
6. Has the appearance been published, exhibited, sold, shown to others, or uploaded? Record dates and evidence.
7. Are the views inventor-supplied and do they show the same product/version at a consistent scale?

## Product class

Record the product name and the applicable classification hint from the official classification material. Do not infer a class from a marketing name alone.

## Brief description

Collect:

- product name;
- use;
- design points;
- designated view that best shows the design points;
- omitted views and why, where applicable;
- color protection statement, where applicable;
- similar-design/basic-design relationship, where applicable.

Describe appearance only. Keep product performance, internal structure, and marketing language out of the design brief.

## View list

Build the view list from the faces involved by the design points. Use the inventor's photographs or drawings; the AI organizes and checks them but does not invent a view or redraw the product. Check:

- every required face/area is represented;
- all views show the same product and version;
- scale, orientation, background, and labeling are consistent;
- the protected appearance is clear;
- omitted views have an explicit record;
- the designated view is included in the final list.

## Output

Write `草稿/申请信息.md`, `草稿/简要说明.md`, and `草稿/视图清单.md`. The filing branch contains the request plus the supplied pictures/photographs and brief description; it does not contain claims, a technical specification, or an abstract.
